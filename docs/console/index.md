# Pluggedspace Console — Agentic Operating System

**Pluggedspace Console** is an enterprise **Agentic Operating System** designed to deploy, orchestrate, and supervise an autonomous fleet of AI agents. Whether managing customer support, scraping market intelligence, automating marketing campaigns, or auditing financial operations, Pluggedspace Console provides the infrastructure, tools, and safety guardrails your team needs.

### 1. Specialized Pre-Built & Custom Agents
- **Monica (Customer Support)**: Autonomous web chat agent with human escalation and domain-locked widget deployment.
- **Marketer (Growth & CRM)**: Automates multi-channel campaigns, lead nurturing, and social messaging.
- **Business Finder (Market Intelligence)**: Discovers prospective leads, company profiles, and competitive intelligence.
- **Finance & Governance**: Audits invoices, vendor compliance, and financial runway projections.
- **Tenant Agent Builder**: Create custom agents tailored to your exact business workflows with configurable prompts, tool scopes, and trigger keywords.

### 2. 28+ Built-in Tools & Modular Skills
Agents interact with your existing business ecosystem through native tools across communication, CRM, web automation, databases, files, and payments:
- **Communication**: Email, Slack, WhatsApp, In-App Notifications.
- **Data & Files**: Database queries, Excel, PDF extraction, OCR, Web Search & Scraper.
- **Business & CRM**: CRM records, Payments (Stripe/Coinbase), Calculations, Automated Reports.
- **Development & Operations**: GitHub, Browser automation, Calendar, Support Tickets.
- **Reusable Skills**: Package multi-step capabilities (e.g., *WebsiteAuditor*, *SEOAnalyzer*, *InvoiceExtractor*) and attach them to any agent.

### 3. Memory Bank (Context & RAG)
- Ingest company documents (`.pdf`, `.docx`, `.txt`, `.csv`) into your workspace's semantic knowledge base.
- Agents dynamically retrieve accurate, up-to-date company policies, pricing sheets, and product specs when formulating responses.

### 4. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) & Policy Guardrails
- **Autonomy Modes**: Run agents in **Manual** (all actions require human sign-off), **Hybrid** (autonomous for routine queries, approvals required for high-risk actions), or **Autonomous** mode.
- **The Approval Inbox**: High-risk actions (refunds, large transfers, mass emails) are routed directly to team members with AI confidence scores and reasoning for one-click approval or rejection.
- **Policy Rules**: Configure fine-grained spending limits, rate limits, and time windows.

### 5. Agent Marketplace
- Browse, install, and review verified agent packages and workflow templates created by the community.
- Publish your own custom agent workflows to share across your organization or the wider ecosystem.

### 6. Real-Time Observability & Telemetry
- Monitor execution latencies, tool health, and task completion in real-time.
- Live task stream and emergency stop controls give you complete oversight over your autonomous fleet.

---

## Enterprise Security & Privacy

- **Tenant Isolation**: Complete data and memory isolation ensures organizational boundaries are strictly enforced.
- **Domain Locking**: Embed chat widgets securely on verified company domains.
- **Audit Trails**: Every agent decision, tool execution, and approval is logged with full lineage for compliance.
